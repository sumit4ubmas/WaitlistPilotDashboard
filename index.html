Waitlist entries

  • Times are shown in each location's configured time zone, named in the location header.
  • To refresh the report, choose Upload new export (or drag the file onto the page) and pick a newer Waitlist export .xlsx. It is read in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere; your browser remembers the last upload until you choose Back to built-in data.
  • Wait times over 3 hours are treated as entries closed late by staff (for example, seated the next day) and left out of wait statistics. They still count as seated.
  • "Hide test entries" removes guests named Test, CHK, New Guest and similar QA names. Guest names are shortened to first name and last initial; phone numbers and emails are not included.
  • Quoted wait is the estimate the guest was shown when joining. Some web check-ins have no quote recorded.
